Why Scholar Hub

Global platforms like Common App solve "which university should I apply to" — for American students, applying to American schools. Nothing like that exists for Rwanda.

Scholarship information here is scattered across government bodies, NGOs, and individual universities, and students often can't tell which scholarships even apply to their school. Scholar Hub aggregates it, verifies it, and links every scholarship to the exact institutions it's valid for — so no opportunity goes unclaimed simply because a student never heard about it.
